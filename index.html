Custom Curtains & Complete Home Solutions

Made-to-measure window treatments and practical home repair solutions for condos, apartments and landed homes across Klang Valley.

SK Curtain & Home Maintenance Services provides custom-made curtains, blinds and general home maintenance services. For curtains and blinds, we discuss your requirements, arrange measurements where required, and provide a quotation before you confirm.
