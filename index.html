Frequently asked questions

Before you get started

Some helpful details about ordering custom curtains and blinds.

Do you provide custom-made curtains?

Yes. We arrange curtains according to your window measurements, selected fabric and preferred style. The process includes on-site measurement, fabric selection, tailoring and installation.

Can you visit my home to take measurements?

Yes, you can contact us to arrange a measurement visit. Share your location and preferred timing on WhatsApp so we can confirm availability.

How much do custom curtains cost?

Pricing depends on window size, fabric, curtain style, number of layers, tracks or accessories, and installation requirements. As a broad planning guide, many home curtain and blind projects may fall around RM1,500–RM5,000; your actual quotation will depend on your requirements.

How long does the order take?

Lead time depends on fabric availability, order size, tailoring and installation scheduling. We will advise the estimated timeline after measurements and order confirmation.

Do you install roller blinds and zebra blinds?

Yes. We provide selected sunscreen roller blinds and zebra blinds. Send us a window photo and approximate measurements to discuss suitable options.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, Cheras, Kajang, Balakong, Seri Kembangan, Puchong, Bukit Jalil, Sunway, Subang Jaya, Ampang, Bangi, Klang and nearby areas. Contact us to confirm availability for your location.

How can I get a quotation?

WhatsApp us at 016-812 1996. If possible, include your location, window photos, approximate width and height, and the style you are interested in.
